## Scope and mode
The whole site, a single page (`index.html`). Mode: Experience. The artifact leads and the interface recedes into it.

## Audience, job, action
People who looked Alex up, plus curious technical visitors. Their job is to find out who he is without being handed a résumé. The action is to add anything up on the machine. Every total earns the next pencilled line of the bio, and tearing off the tape reveals the full bio and links on its back.

## Proof and content
The first-person professional bio from PRODUCT.md, split into four pencil notes and the full note on the back. The only links are GitHub, LinkedIn, and email. No metrics beyond "hundreds of finance users".

## Direction and memorable moment
Proof Tape: a working printing calculator on a felt desk blotter. The machine does the math and a person signs off. The moment to remember is the pencil auditing your total with a tick and writing who Alex is beside it.

## Constraints
Static files on GitHub Pages with no external services. Sound is synthesized and off by default. The skip link and the tear button reach the bio by keyboard. Reduced motion removes feed, writing, and flight animation.

## Open decisions
Alex's real handwriting could replace the Nanum Pen Script face. The live Pages branch (`quiet`) is not updated by this work.
